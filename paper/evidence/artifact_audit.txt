The graph-temporal block contains six signals. For each signal it stores five
aggregate spatial/peak descriptors, four statistics for each of 28 fixed PMU pairs,
and two nominal weighted summaries: 6 * (5 + 28 * 4 + 2) = 714.

The production call to extract_dynamic_features_from_frames enables rolling,
rls_kalman, and graph_temporal blocks without passing topology_distances. In the
extractor, missing pair distances default to 1.0. The selected schema contains no
GSP candidate, RESID candidate, or effective-impedance candidate columns. The paper
therefore describes these 714 variables as cross-PMU spatial-temporal summaries,
not topology-weighted candidate evidence.
